How Often Each Borough Refuses

Hover over or tap a borough.

Refusal rate = refused ÷ (approved + refused), for applications validated . Grey boroughs have fewer than 30 decisions of that type. Where the application form gave no type, the classifier below filled it in. Applications decided by the two development corporations (LLDC and OPDC) are not shown. Barking and Dagenham's data records decisions mostly for householder and full planning applications, so its rate for all applications leans towards those two types.

Similar Past Decisions

    The chance of refusal if the application is decided, from a logistic regression trained on 2019–2025 London decisions (ROC-AUC 0.76; the riskiest tenth were refused three times as often as average). It sees only the description, borough and type, not drawings, objections or policy: a guide, not a verdict.

    Similar past decisions are the decided applications of the same type in the same borough whose descriptions share the most words and word pairs with yours (TF-IDF). Dates are when each was validated; links go to the council's record where the Datahub has one.
